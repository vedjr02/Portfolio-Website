# Product

## What this is
Personal portfolio for Vedant Ambre, a business analyst finishing a Master's in Business Analytics at Maynooth who also ships working software. The site sells evidence: a shipped macOS app, sourced case studies, and data products that hold up with stakeholders.

## Who it is for
Hiring managers and recruiters evaluating business analyst candidates. Secondary: founders or analytics leads looking for someone who can scope a problem and build the fix.

## Jobs to be done
1. Understand within one viewport that Vedant is a BA who ships software, with Hold My Code as the proof.
2. Scan every project quickly. The main page lists them compactly; details open in Quick Look, off the main flow.
3. Open a live demo or get in touch without hunting (Contact in the menu bar, dock and hero).

## Positioning
Business analyst who ships products. Target: BA roles, available from Sep 2026 (Master's end). Ireland-based. Flagship: Hold My Code (macOS menu bar app, holdmycode.xyz; source is private, releases are public). Case studies (NVIDIA, Starbucks, AdFlex/SEI) come second. Do not invent work-authorization claims or app usage numbers; quote only repo and release facts.

## Brand / voice
Plain, specific, calm. Specific over clever. No startup-slop jargon. The visual world is a Mac desktop in light appearance (see DESIGN.md).

## Non-goals
Not a SaaS landing page. Not a designer portfolio. Not a résumé clone. No purple AI aesthetic.

## Success
Visitor opens Hold My Code or a project in Quick Look, and leaves with one next action: email or a live demo.
